Red Flag Patterns

🚨 Common Cyber Red Flags

Stay Suspicious
Act Now or Your Account Will Be BlockedUrgency and fear are often used to pressure quick decisions.
You Won a Prize!Unexpected rewards usually require verification, payment, or personal data.
Share Your OTPNo legitimate service should request a one-time password for account verification.
Send Money for VerificationVerification or refund requests should never require you to send money.
Click This Urgent LinkUrgent link requests often hide fake websites or malware files.
Install This App for SupportUnknown apps may be remote-access tools or malware.
Guaranteed Investment ReturnsHigh guaranteed returns are a classic warning sign.
Your KYC Has ExpiredScammers use financial or identity pressure to steal documents or money.
Your SIM Will Be BlockedUrgent SIM or telecom claims are often false, used to harvest data.
Your Package Is StuckBank or delivery messages should include clear official details and context.
Password Security

🔐 Password Security

Identity Protection
  • Use a strong passwordUse at least 12–16 characters with upper and lower case, numbers, and symbols.
  • Unique password for every accountNever reuse a password across personal, bank, email, or shopping accounts.
  • Use a password managerStore passwords securely and generate unique, complex passwords.
  • Enable MFAAdd a second factor like authenticator apps, hardware keys, or recovery codes.
  • PasskeysUse passkeys when supported for stronger, password-free sign-in.
  • Avoid common mistakesDo not write passwords in notebooks, shared notes, or reuse birthdays and pet names.
🔑 Password Strength Tips
Password strength
12+ length Unique Never reuse
Use a passphrase that is memorable but not personal.
Store recovery email and phone carefully.
Do not share passwords over calls or messages.
Set up MFA and update recovery details.
OTP, UPI & Payment Safety

💳 OTP, UPI & Payment Safety

Before You Pay
  • Never share OTPOTP is a verification code. Do not share it with anyone, including fake support agents.
  • Never share your UPI PINUPI PIN should never be revealed for any reason.
  • Bank never asks for UPI PIN to receive moneyReceiving payments should never require your UPI PIN.
  • Never approve unknown collect requestsCheck the recipient before approving any payment or request.
  • QR codes can make paymentsScan only after previewing the payment destination and verifying the amount.
  • Verify payment screenshotsDo not trust screenshots alone as proof of payment.
Before You Pay Checklist
Social Media Safety

📱 Social Media Safety

Public Privacy
  • Keep accounts privateUse privacy controls where appropriate and limit public access.
  • Avoid oversharingDo not post your full address, phone, family details, or routine location openly.
  • Do not share live locationNever broadcast location updates publicly or in groups you do not trust.
  • Beware of fake profilesCheck friend requests and profile details before responding.
  • Report suspicious accountsReport impersonation, harassment, fake investment requests, and suspicious DMs.
Social Media Hygiene
Use strong recovery email and phone.
Enable 2FA and review active sessions.
Do not click suspicious DMs or shortened links.
Review who follows and interacts with your account.
Email & Message Safety

📧 Email & Message Safety

Message Hygiene
  • Check sender addressesLook closely for suspicious domains, first-name variations, and fake email hosts.
  • Be careful with urgent languageThreats and pressure are common for scam email and SMS.
  • Do not open unknown attachmentsLinks or attachments from unknown senders may contain malware or fake forms.
  • Watch fake invoices and delivery messagesScammers use fake invoices, coupon, and shipping updates to send links.
  • Read spelling and domain detailsWatch for typos, extra words, or strange brand variations in the email address.
SAFE vs SUSPICIOUS Examples
SAFE FraudLens Support: update your account settings from your trusted app.
SUSPICIOUS Urgent bank verification — click now or lose access.
Device Security

💻 Device Security

Protected Devices
  • Keep OS updatedInstall trusted operating system updates and vendor security patches.
  • Update browsersKeep your browser current and configure safe defaults.
  • Install only trusted appsUse official app stores and avoid APK downloads or cracked software.
  • Use screen lock and encryptionProtect your phone or laptop with a biometric or PIN screen lock.
  • Backup important dataKeep backups of important files and documents.
Device Safety actions
Do not use unknown USB devices.

Public Wi-Fi Safety

📶 Public Wi-Fi Safety

Network Awareness
  • Verify the network nameConfirm the official Wi-Fi network name before connecting.
  • Avoid sensitive transactionsDo not make online payments or share OTPs on public or unknown networks.
  • Turn off auto-connectDisable automatic joining to unknown Wi-Fi networks.
  • Use HTTPSEnsure the websites you visit use secure HTTPS and trusted domains.
  • Forget unused networksRemove networks you no longer use to reduce future exposure.
Public Wi-Fi Rules
Use your mobile hotspot when possible for sensitive tasks.
Avoid sending passwords or payments over public Wi-Fi.
Use trusted VPN only if you know the service.
Do not accept a network login page without checking the location.
Online Shopping Safety

🛒 Online Shopping Safety

Purchase Awareness
  • Check website and domainLook for spelling, secure address, return policy, and trusted branding.
  • Avoid unbelievable discountsVery low prices can indicate fake shops or counterfeit goods.
  • Check seller informationSearch reviews and verify the business address and contact details.
  • Use trusted payment methodsPrefer methods with dispute support rather than payments made by advance transfer.
  • Do not download APKs from messagesFake shopping messages may send APK files or payment apps.
Shopping Rules
Do not share OTPs unnecessarily.
Review return, refund, and delivery policy.
Never send money before confirming seller identity.
Check if the seller uses an official website or store.
Job Scam Safety

💼 Job Scam Safety

Before Accepting a Job Offer
  • Verify the companySearch for the real HR handle, official email domain, and company profile.
  • Do not pay registration feesScam offers ask for security deposit, training, or legal fee payments.
  • Beware fake work-from-home offersWatch for offers that ask you to install unknown apps or software.
  • Never share OTP or bank credentialsFake interviews ask for OTP, bank credentials, or sensitive personal data.
  • Offer letter verificationVerify documents, company address, and real recruitment communication.
Before Accepting a Job Offer

📈 Investment Scam Awareness

High Risk
  • Guaranteed returnsHigh guaranteed returns are a major warning sign.
  • Fake trading platformsUse trusted financial education and verified official investment resources.
  • Fake crypto or investment groupsDo not trust strangers or groups that pressure you to invest quickly.
  • Impersonation of financial expertsVerify credentials from official sources rather than social profiles.

Scan Before You Trust

  • Verify payment recipientsAlways check the displayed beneficiary or payment flow before sending money.
  • Never enter sensitive infoNever type passwords, OTPs, or payment details on an unverified site.
